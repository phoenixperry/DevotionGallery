319 Scholes and Devotion Gallery present: The Shift

5 August 2010

A three day benefit celebrating one year of curation in the 11206 Brooklyn zipcode
August 27th, 8pm through Sunday August 29th at 8pm

THE SHIFT is a birthday benefit for Devotion Gallery and 319 Scholes. It includes an exhibition, art auction with all pieces capped at $250, multiple performances, high calibre live music, and workshops focused on music technology. If you missed the last year at these two ground breaking, kick-ass exhibition spaces, or want a sneak peak at what is in store for 2011, THE SHIFT is not to be missed.
PARTICIPATING ARTISTS: SOFTlab, Dan Tesene, Marius Watz, Benton Bainbridge, Chris Jordan, Joshue Ott, Bethany Shorb, Sougwen Chung, Aaron Meyers, Jorden Haley, David Last, Phoenix Perry, Micah Frank,
PARTICIPATING ARTISTS: Lindsy Howard, Michelle Mayer, Duncan Malaschock, Alexandra Gorczynski, Dave Linnenbank
FRIDAY NIGHT LINE UP IN ORDER OF Appearance: 8pm - 4am | Silent Art Auction of select works starts at 8pm, Tū, Zach Layton, Bora Yoon, Black Swan, Columboid, Sepalcure, Dethlab, Todd Pendu, Mike Dextro
SATURDAY NIGHT LINE UP IN ORDER OF Appearance: 7pm until 4am | Sindy Butz, Ted Hayes, Ezekiel Honig, RJ Valeo, Igal Nassima & Taylan Sinan Yilmaz
SUNDAY: 319 Scholes brings artists and audiences together with culinary creations. Food is served from 3-5pm.SATURDAY AND SUNDAY WORKSHOPS ORGANIZED BY DEVOTION: Max Fuel / Max4Live, Ableton: 101, Ableton: Advanced Seminar, DIY Electroacoustics: Pickups and SuperCollider. (note class admission not included in ticket price or vise versa)

$10 per night - $25 3 day pass - Free Saturday and Sunday during gallery hours (12pm - 4pm)
LOCATION: 319 Scholes, Brooklyn, NY, 11206 (L to Morgan)
