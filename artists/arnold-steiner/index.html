Artist Statement

Set in a future world, Modern Romance is a meditation on humanity quest for perfection. This post apocalyptic vision show an extreme time where people have sacrificed their humanity in the ultimate quest for freedom from death. The blissful robots dancing celebrate the end of the human spirit and the biological world. Both seductive and repulsive, this image toys with our false hopes a technologically better future.

Bio

Arnold Steiner is an artist living and working in Miami, FL. His work is a complex collision of the technological and natural worlds. As a designer, Surface magazine rated him one of the 10 most important of his generation. He has designed award winning record covers for Tipper, Venetian Snares, Otto Von Schirach, A Guy Called Gerald, Phoenecia and hundreds of others. His photo realistic renderings of surreal landscapes on countless club flyers, posters and tee-shirts have has influenced designers worldwide. He has just released two new compilation fine art books with a third on the way. As a musician, he runs his own record label, Transient Force and releases music under the name AS1.
