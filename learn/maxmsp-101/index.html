Max/MSP 101

2 October 2010

Max is a powerful graphic programming environment with a huge community of users. Start with an idea and then build that idea into performance tools or utilities not available in existing software. Learn how to manipulate data, MIDI, sound, and even video by connecting various boxes together - no coding necessary.  This course also serves as a prerequisite for Max for Live courses at Devotion.

  • Dates:  TBA
  • When: This course meets 6 times, Wed-Thurs from 11 am - 1:30 pm.
  • Where: 54 Maujer, Brooklyn, NY 11206
  • Level: Beginner
  • Prerequisite: Intermediate Mac OS X or Windows end user skills. Some computer audio experience is highly recommended.
  • Student Discounts available - email us for info.
  • Tuition: $450  - Please read our Registration & Cancellation Policy
Topics Covered:

  • Message types in Max

  • Review of the MIDI spec for control systems

  • Planning your system

  • Encapsulation and modularity

  • Building standalone applications

  • Optimization for performance

  • More...

About the instructor:
Dave Linnenbank is an electronic musician by nature and an educator by trade.  His involvement with Puremagnetik.com led to the recently released "Max Fuel, the First", a bundle of plugin-like devices for Ableton Live.  Dave also teaches at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) in Massachusetts, and is working on a book about sound synthesis with Tom Rhea.
