Ableton 303 - From Idea to Reality; Making Live Yours

2 October 2010

This course teaches you how to efficiently make use of Live’s amazingly customizable feature set, so quick realization of complex ideas is fun and efficient. To truly take advantage of Live’s power, you must learn how to combine multiple features into single workflows.
From custom mapping techniques, to building your own drums racks, to creative signal flow for effects processing, we will traverse a broad spectrum of features as you push your Live skills towards advanced territory. By the end of this course, you should be able to translate what’s in your head into sonic reality faster than before.

Topics Covered:

  • Advanced MIDI, Key, & Macro Mapping

  • Dummy Clips - Audio & MIDI

  • Advanced Signal Flow

  • Advanced Signal Processing

  • Instrument Rack Master Class

  • Drum Rack Master Class

  • Non-obvious uses of Live Devices

About the instructor:

Brian Jackson, MA is Devotion's Director of Education and one of its co-founders. He is an internationally released electronic musician, professional audio engineer, and highly experienced teacher. One of the very first Ableton Certified Trainers on the planet, his expertise also encompasses Pro Tools, Logic, synthesis, and audio mastering. Brian received his MA in East-West Psychology from the California Institute of Integral Studies in 1999.
