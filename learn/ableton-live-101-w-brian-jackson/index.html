Ableton Live 101 w/ Brian Jackson

1 October 2010

Ableton Live is fun, powerful, flexible, spontaneous, and elegant - but often confounding to new users. There are just a few concepts required for understanding what Live is about, and this course covers all of Live’s fundamental concepts. Ableton Live 101 is for musicians and DJs that are new to Ableton Live. It covers all of the essentials to get you up and running fast so that you start having fun and making music right away. This course also serves as a prerequisite for numerous other Ableton courses at Devotion.

    About the instructor:
    Brian Jackson, MA is Devotion's Director of Education and one of its co-founders. He is an internationally released electronic musician, professional audio engineer, and highly experienced teacher. One of the very first Ableton Certified Trainers on the planet, his expertise also encompasses Pro Tools, Logic, synthesis, and audio mastering. Brian received his MA in East-West Psychology from the California Institute of Integral Studies in 1999.
