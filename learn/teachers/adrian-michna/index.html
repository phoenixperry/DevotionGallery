Adrian Michna

2 October 2010


Ghostly International recording artist Adrian Michna began studying trombone in 1988 in 4th grade. From 1990-1996 he participated in NYSSMA and the annual All County Orchestra in Westchester, NY.

In 2000 he graduated from University of Miami with a Bachelors degree in Music Business & Entertainment Industries.  In 2002 he moved to Vienna Austria and became the trombone instructor at The American International School.


In 2003 he moved to Brooklyn, and gave private lessons on trombone/music theory/Logic Audio and later participated in music lectures at Eyebeam Art and Technology Center in NYC, and The Museum of Contemporary Art in North Miami, FL.

Since 2005 he has toured as a DJ and performed live all over the world. Festival and club highlights have been:

Sonar - Barcelona, Spain Decibel Fest - Seattle, Washington Berghain - Berlin, Germany DEMF - Detroit, Michigan Bon Bon - Shanghai, China Club Vodka - Moscow, Russia Suss Fel Nap - Budapest, Hungary Rothbury - Rothbury Michigan Mezzanine - San Francisco, CA Museum of Natural History - NY, NY Brooklyn Museum - Brooklyn, NY
