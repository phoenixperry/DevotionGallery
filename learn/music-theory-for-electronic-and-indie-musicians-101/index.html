Music Theory for Electronic and Indie Musicians 101

2 October 2010

This course is for any musician, producer, or DJ - electronic or otherwise - that is not formally trained. All of the fundamentals of rhythm, melody, and harmony are covered. If you want to write catchier melodies, program drums, or just gain a better understanding of the language of music, then this course is for you. Musical examples from many styles and genres, classic and contemporary, are utilized to make theory concrete.

  • Basic Rhythm Theory
  • Syncopation
  • Polyrhythms
  • Notes
  • Chords
  • Scales
  • Song Structures
  • Melody and Harmony
  • More...
About the instructor: Flavio Gaete, MM has degrees from Boston University (MM, Viola Performance) and Berklee College of Music (BM, Music Synthesis). Flavio is a violist and electronic musician. He performs regularly in both classical and pop music, his music works range from sound installations to music for film and dance and he teaches courses on MIDI, post-production and digital audio in the New York City area.
