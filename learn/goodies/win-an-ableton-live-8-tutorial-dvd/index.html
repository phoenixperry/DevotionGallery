Win an Ableton Live 8 Tutorial DVD

2 October 2010

Sign up for a class by October 15th and you will be entered in a drawing - with great odds too. You will win a copy of the Ableton Live 8 Course Clips Master DVD by Devotion Learn's own Brian Jackson. Also, Brian will give you some of the example Sets used in the creation of the movie tutorials, so you can follow along. Get ready to master the key operational techniques and cool new features of Ableton Live 8! Ableton Live 8 Course Clips Master is a hands-on training DVD-ROM that provides you with a unique way to take your music to the next level with Live. If you've been using Live and you want to get more out of your rig, then you've come to the right place. You'll cover the major topics as you work through more than five hours of movie tutorials.
If you're a new user and need to get up to speed with Live, use the Introduction topic to take you through set-up, basic definition, and the new features added in Live 8. Ableton Live 8 Course Clips Master is full of movie tutorials that include not only basic functionality, but also production techniques, special shortcuts, key commands, and before and after audio examples.
