Synthesis 101 - From Virtual To Vintage Analog

3 October 2010

This course is a must for any aspiring electronic musician. Learn the history of the synthesizer and its path in both experimental and popular music.  We will discuss how sounds and features developed, and how they shaped the music they appeared in.  
Investigate the basic building blocks of synthesis and how you can create and control your own unique sounds, while you gain a better understanding of sound in general.  In addition to software synths from Logic, Max/MSP and d16 you will gain some hands on experience with classic vintage analog synths, such as the Korg MS-20 and the Sequential Circuits Prophet 5.
  • Dates: Monday Oct 18th - Tuesday Nov
  • When: This course meets 8 times on Mon and Tues from 2 pm - 4:30 pm.
  • Where: 54 Maujer, Brooklyn, NY 11206
  • Level: Beginner
  • Prerequisite: Basic knowledge of Mac OS X and some experience as a musician.
  • Student Discounts available - email us for info.

Topics Covered:

  • Hands-on with classic analog synths!

  • Types of synthesis

  • History of synthesizers

  • Common uses of synths

  • Common synthesis parameters

  • How to make different sounds from scratch.

  • Fundamentals of Sound

  • More...


About the instructor: Matthew Patterson Curry has been immersed in all things audio for the past 14 years, starting with sound installations at San Francisco Art Institute, where he was initially introduced to Max/MSP.  This took him to work at Cycling '74 for 5 years doing technical support and testing while he lead a parallel life producing his electronic "dork pop" under the moniker Safety Scissors.  After a 3 year stint in Berlin, performing all over the world he returned to San Francisco where he focused on audio post production, mainly working for the Emmy award winning TV station Current TV.  He has taught courses and workshops, on Max/MSP, Logic and Pro Tools, at universities and small schools, even wearing the headset microphone at the Apple Store a couple of times.
