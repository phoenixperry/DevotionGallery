Ableton Live 202 - Record, Edit, Mix

2 October 2010

Once you have all of Live’s fundamentals under your belt, its time to dig deeper. This intermediate level course covers various audio and MIDI recording techniques for stage and studio. After capturing your performances, edit and arrange them to finish your idea.

We’ll cover methods for creating effective arrangements in Live, and explore how arranging blurs into the mixing process. Finally, you will learn the in and outs of EQ, Reverb, Delay, and Compression - including side-chaining techniques - as you learn how to complete a final mixdown using Live’s powerful mixing capabilities.

About the instructor:

Brian Jackson, MA is Devotion's Director of Education and one of its co-founders. He is an internationally released electronic musician, professional audio engineer, and highly experienced teacher. One of the very first Ableton Certified Trainers on the planet, his expertise also encompasses Pro Tools, Logic, synthesis, and audio mastering. Brian received his MA in East-West Psychology from the California Institute of Integral Studies in 1999.
