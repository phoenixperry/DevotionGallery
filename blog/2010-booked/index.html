2010 booked!

2 February 2010

Our 2010 calendar is finalized and we have some amazing work coming up in the coming months. Here's a quick preview of what you can expect.

Feb 19 -28th Stephen Cardinale presents Photos from Unsound Festival

March 12 - April 4 Soft Lab will be building an architectural installation. Details soon

April 9th - 18th Joshue Ott presents Multi User Art. Artists: Anton Marini (Vade), Peter Kirn, Joshue Ott (superDraw), Eric Redlinger, Chris Jordan (SeeJ)

Workshops on Mrmr will be part of this show details coming soon

May 7th – May 30th: ScrapCycle(reUSE/reCOMBINE) is an upcycled exhibition and performance series featuring works made via reuse and recombination. From quilting to computation, the concepts of reuse and recombination take many forms. In addition to presenting these concrete implementations, the exhibition serves to liken the small pervasive effects of social sculpture and environmental activism to a fine-tuning of interdependent parameters with global results. (Artists include Ari Tabei, Katherine Liberovskaya + o.blaat (Keiko Uenishi), LoVid, Michael Dory, Phillip Stearns, Pollie Barden, Ranjit Bhatnagar, and Torino:Margolis. Presented by Resident Not-for-Profit, Analogous Projects.)

Jun 2nd – June 27th: Discrete Interactions / Analogous Interactions is an exhibition of discretized complexity, and features works from ICMC 2010. (Artists include Brett Balogh, Douglas Repetto, Nick Lesley, and Philip Galanter. Presented by Resident Not-for-Profit, Analogous Projects.)

July 2 – July 11 free103point9 AIRtime Exhibition: Zach Poff's "Radio Silence"

July 16th - July 25th free103point9 AIRtime Exhibition: Grufatt/Brown's "Bike Box"

July 30th - Aug 16th free103point9 AIRtime Exhibition: Brett Balough's "Noospherium"

Aug 27 - Sept 26 Dan Tesene Solo Show

Oct 1 - Oct 31 Paul Prudence, Marius Watz and Aaron Meyers

Nov 2nd - Dec 19th Art Games. Artist list coming soon
